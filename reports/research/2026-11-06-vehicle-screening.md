## Probe result

GitHub Actions run 42206543824 acquired and rendered the three candidates. The reviewer individually inspected each 12-view contact sheet and recorded decisions in `reviews/vehicle-probe-01.json`, with retained contact sheets in `reports/rejected/vehicle-probe-01/`. All three were rejected for the current quality goal: the jet has simplified windows/cockpit and smooth toy-like surfaces; the tugboat has a sparse deck and blocky wheelhouse; the motorcycle has a detailed frame but simplified engine/body and synthetic-looking finish, with eight reported zero-area triangles. All three imported, had zero glTF errors, and had 2K embedded images, but resolution alone did not make them photorealistic. The tug had ten validator warnings. No upstream Blender actions or library-authored animation clips were detected; interiors and working mechanics were not verified. These rejections leave the approved total at 333, still 117 short of 450. Continue researching rather than count these as additions.
