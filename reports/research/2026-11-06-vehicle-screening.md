## Probe result

GitHub Actions run 42206543824 acquired and rendered the three candidates. The reviewer individually inspected each 12-view contact sheet and recorded decisions in `reviews/vehicle-probe-01.json`, with retained contact sheets in `reports/rejected/vehicle-probe-01/`. All three were rejected for the current quality goal: the jet has simplified windows/cockpit and smooth toy-like surfaces; the tugboat has a sparse deck and blocky wheelhouse; the motorcycle has a detailed frame but simplified engine/body and synthetic-looking finish, with eight reported zero-area triangles. All three imported, had zero glTF errors, and had 2K embedded images, but resolution alone did not make them photorealistic. The tug had ten validator warnings. No upstream Blender actions or library-authored animation clips were detected; interiors and working mechanics were not verified. These rejections leave the approved total at 333, still 117 short of 450. Continue researching rather than count these as additions.


## Continuation: sailing ships and source probe

Poly Haven offers related Dutch ships and a separate pinnace. Following individual 12-view review, `ph-dutch-ship-large-01` and `ph-ship-pinnace` were approved as two distinctive static exteriors; large 02 was rejected as too visually similar to large 01. The approved models use 1K maps, and neither has verified interiors, armatures or animation. The GLB/glTF validator had zero errors and three or seven warnings respectively. See the review JSON and retained rejection sheet for specifics.

3DAssets.dev is an additional source lead: its license page explicitly permits unmodified model resale under CC0 while warning that third-party rights are excluded, and historic items can differ. API metadata for prospective original-contributor candidates explicitly discloses AI generation; sampled car, helicopter, airliner and bicycle candidates often have **zero textures** despite their `realistic` tag. Upstream claims of clips or cabin detail require actual image and motion inspection. The adapter restricts intake to individually published CC0 models from the platform's own contributor and preserves source metadata; it was unit-tested and live-tested in Actions run 42208968783. Four individually reviewed candidates (sports car, helicopter, touring bicycle and commuter bicycle) were rejected as too simplified or insufficiently textured for this intake. Motion and interiors were not inspected because the car and helicopter already failed visual quality. Retained sheets and the formal decisions are under `reports/rejected/vehicle-3dassets-probe-01/` and `reviews/vehicle-3dassets-probe-01.json`. This is one source, not many independent sources. OpenGameArt creator leads are also unreviewed; count them only after item-level checks. The catalog is now 335, 115 shy of 450; the >50 independent-source request is still unmet.
