# Animation-rule finding — 2026-11-06

The `wheel_roll` substring matcher mistakenly matched the single combined mesh
`wheelchair_01` in a newly acquired Poly Haven wheelchair. The three generated
inspection frames showed the **whole chair rotating**, not its wheels. The model
also lacks visible footrests. It remains quarantined; the autogenerated animation
is not a usable wheel animation. Rules now match `wheel` as a delimited node token,
with regression tests for `wheelchair_01`, `flywheel_rotor`, Grab3D's separate
wheel nodes and Kenney's hyphenated nodes. No claim is made that name-based rules
alone prove correct rotation axes, pivots or motion: review the actual frames.

The existing published `g3-flywheel` has one legacy `wheel_roll` clip targeting
`flywheel_rotor`. That is a rotor spin with a misleading road-wheel name, not a
separate road-wheel action. Its limitation is now exposed in the catalog. Other
published `wheel_roll` metadata identifies separate wheel nodes, but this check
alone does not verify their actual animation behavior.
