# Full outfit classification and review — 2026-11-07

A complete outfit for this collection means coordinated body clothing (a one-piece garment or upper and lower clothing) **plus footwear in the same asset**. Fit, unobstructed geometry, visual quality, CC0 provenance and meaningful difference from existing outfits must be reviewed separately; presence of components alone is not approval. Headwear and accessories are included when essential to the depicted role. Gender labels describe the source/design presentation, not a restriction on who may wear the clothing. “Historical” refers to period clothing, not the wearer's age. These are disjoint primary navigation groups: occupational outfits go under work, casual under casual, and other modern outfits under modern. Empty groups are deliberately shown at zero rather than filled with unverified assets.
